Curriculum Vitae

I am Thodoris Tsilivis (Θοδωρής Τσιλιβής), a third-year Ph.D. student in Computing and Data Science at Boston University. My research interests lie in algorithmic game theory, mechanism design, and randomized algorithms. My Ph.D. is advised by Professor Kira Goldner.

I received my joint BSc & MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National Technical University of Athens. For my thesis, "Learning Augmented Combinatorial Auctions", I had the privilege of being advised by Professor Dimitris Fotakis (NTUA) and Professor Vasilis Gkatzelis (Drexel University).
